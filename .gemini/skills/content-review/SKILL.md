# Content Review

## Inputs
The item ID, plus a preview (scene iframe URL with `?day=60`, an audio file, or both for a template).

## Checklist
**All items**
- [ ] Fits the mood: "ink on warm paper, breathing slowly". No action, no tension.
- [ ] Tags are honest (1–5, from `docs/content/TAGS.md`).
- [ ] The name is evocative and exists in both EN and VI.

**Music**
- [ ] 50–85 BPM, no vocals, no harsh drums.
- [ ] The seam is inaudible over 3 loops on headphones.
- [ ] Loudness is about −16 LUFS, with no clipping.

**Scene**
- [ ] No visible reset across 2 day cycles. No stuck or popping figures.
- [ ] Slow motion (≤ 1 cycle per 2s). At most 6 figures.
- [ ] Readable when cropped to 9:16. The night palette glows warmly.

**Template**
- [ ] Shares ≥ 2 tags (1 is acceptable only with a written reason).
- [ ] Time of day and instrument match (see the creative brief pairing rules).

## Verdict
`APPROVE` → the admin may publish. `REVISE` → give at most 5 concrete notes (what to change, and why). `REJECT` → one-line reason. Record the verdict in the item's admin note or in the task handoff.
