# Creative Brief (Owner: Director)

## Mood
"Ink on warm paper, breathing slowly." Everything should feel hand-drawn, quiet, unhurried and slightly nostalgic. Think of a Studio Ghibli background sketch crossed with a lo-fi radio stream.

## Pillars
1. **Stillness with life**: always some small motion (a swaying lantern, a passing bird, rain), never action.
2. **Time passes**: the scenes use the day/night cycle (`daynight.ts`). A full day lasts 10–30 min of real time.
3. **No surprises**: no sudden sounds, flashes or jump cuts. Every change is a fade.
4. **Warm restraint**: an ink and paper palette, with one accent glow at night (windows, lanterns).

## Scene rules
- Viewbox 1920×1080 (16:9). Composition must stay readable when cropped to 9:16 (keep the important content in the centre third).
- At most 6 animated figures. Motion is slow: walk at gait speed × 0.6 or less, nothing faster than 1 cycle per 2s.
- The scene must loop or evolve with no end and no visible reset.
- Pointer interaction is optional and gentle (wind, a ripple).

## Music rules
- 60–85 BPM (sleep: 50–65). No vocals. No drums louder than soft brushes or lo-fi kicks.
- Keys: major or modal, gentle. Nothing dissonant or tense.
- Loudness −16 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dBTP.

## Pairing rules (for templates)
- At least 2 shared tags is ideal, 1 is acceptable.
- Match the time of day: rain or night scenes go with piano or ambient pads. Sunny scenes go with acoustic or lo-fi.
- The Director approves every template before it is published.

## Review checklist (Director sign-off)
- [ ] Fits the mood and the pillars  - [ ] No jarring motion or sound  - [ ] Seam inaudible / reset invisible over 3 loops  - [ ] Tags honest  - [ ] Name evocative, EN+VI
