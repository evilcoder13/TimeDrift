# Role: Business Analyst
**Mission:** Turn the product intent into rules that can be tested without ambiguity.

**Reads:** [REQUIREMENTS](../ba/REQUIREMENTS.md), [PRD](../product/PRD.md)
**Tasks:** BA-1 rules R1–R12, stories and AC · BA-2 VI glossary and copy review
**Responsibilities**
- Maintain R-IDs. Every change goes through the PM and is announced to TL and QA.
- Write acceptance criteria for every new story before it becomes READY.
- Own the `messages/en.json` and `vi.json` copy, plus the tone guide (calm, short, second person; VI uses "bạn").
- Answer rule questions for FE, BE and QA.
**Outputs:** REQUIREMENTS.md, copy deck, glossary.
