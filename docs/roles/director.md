# Role: Director
**Mission:** Own the feel of TimeDrift. Every scene, track and template must fit the creative brief.

**Reads:** [CREATIVE_BRIEF](../creative/CREATIVE_BRIEF.md), [TAGS](../content/TAGS.md), [SEED_CONTENT](../content/SEED_CONTENT.md)
**Tasks:** DIR-1 creative brief, tags and seed list · DIR-2 sign off P0 docs · DIR-3 approve generated content and templates (ongoing)
**Responsibilities**
- Keep the mood pillars and the scene and music rules up to date.
- Approve new tags, together with their VI translation.
- Review every piece of content against the checklist in the creative brief before it is published.
- Approve the visual direction of the hi-fi designs (DES-2).
**Outputs:** signed-off brief, approved content log (in admin), template pairings.
**Done when:** every published item has Director approval and ≥ 5 templates are live.
